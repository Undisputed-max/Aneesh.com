A 5v5 competitive multiplayer shooter where two teams of five players compete to score more points than their opponents. Featuring distinct heroes, unique weapons, and team-based combat, the game focuses on competitive gameplay, strategic map design, and coordinated team play.

My role combined Level Design and Core Gameplay Development in Unreal Engine using C++. I designed all the maps featured in the game's trailer and worked on core gameplay systems, weapon mechanics, and multiplayer gameplay to deliver engaging competitive matches.

// My responsibilities
  • Designed and developed all maps featured in the game trailer — level layout, combat spaces, player navigation, and map flow, with a focus on balanced 5v5 competitive gameplay.
  • Worked as a Core Gameplay Developer — implemented gameplay mechanics, weapon systems, combat interactions, and match flow using Unreal Engine C++.
  • Developed and iterated weapon and combat systems — weapon behaviour, firing mechanics, hit detection, damage handling, and integration with character gameplay.
  • Contributed to 5v5 multiplayer gameplay — worked on gameplay systems supporting team-based combat, player interactions, and competitive match progression.
  • Collaborated across level design and gameplay development — iterated on map layouts, combat encounters, and gameplay mechanics to support engaging team-based matches.
